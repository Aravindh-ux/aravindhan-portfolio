Case study / 01

Service health dashboard

Enterprise internal tooling · Dashboard UX

The challenge

Make system status easy to read quickly.

Operational teams need to know whether critical dependencies are active and when they were last checked. A status view should make the current state obvious without forcing people to interpret scattered signals.

Design direction

Show the answer before the detail.

The proposed dashboard emphasizes a clear active/down state, last-checked time, and a consistent service card structure. The focus is service health only, keeping data discrepancies and unrelated monitoring outside the scope.

Current status: wireframe exploration. Final implementation and measured outcomes have not been claimed.
Case study / 02

A clearer underwriting journey

B2B CRM · Form and feedback states

The challenge

A dead end should still explain what happens next.

When an applicant cannot continue, a generic end state can create uncertainty. The experience needs to communicate the result respectfully, keep the message focused, and help the person understand the next step.

Design direction

Compare layouts around the content.

Three directions were explored for a non-qualification experience: an illustration-led layout, a compact layout, and a content-first variation. The design was refined through feedback, with attention to hierarchy and readability.

Focus: communicating the outcome clearly and consistently. No unverified conversion or completion metrics are claimed.
Concept / 03

Live AI roleplay

AI product concept · Real-time interaction

The challenge

Make a simulated conversation feel live.

A roleplay screen has to support two different needs at once: staying present in the conversation and understanding how the session is going. Too much UI can interrupt practice; too little can leave users unsure what is happening.

Design direction

Keep feedback close, controls easy to reach.

The concept brings the AI persona and scenario into view, uses an audio waveform to signal live speech, keeps transcript access available, and makes pause and exit controls discoverable without dominating the conversation.

Exploratory single-screen concept; no live AI functionality is included in this portfolio page.
Concept / 04

Aiora — in-home care

Healthcare concept · Care coordination

The opportunity

Support a difficult time with calmer experiences.

People coordinating palliative care at home may need to navigate sensitive information, routines, and support from different people. A digital experience should feel reassuring and help make the next step understandable.

Design direction

Bring important tasks into one gentle overview.

The concept explores a focused care overview, plain-language task prompts, and a calmer visual system. The intent is to make coordination feel more manageable while recognizing the sensitivity of the care context.

Concept work. The content on this page does not represent clinical guidance or a deployed healthcare service.
